# Propeller Replacement Requirements

## Current Status

The drone should not be flown with the current generic 1045 replacement propellers

The generic propellers are through-hole props and cannot be safely clamped onto the current short threaded motor shafts with the available nuts

## Required Replacement Style

The replacement propellers need to be threaded-hub or self-locking 1045 propellers compatible with the Holybro S500 V2 motor setup

Search terms:

* Holybro 530084 Propeller1045 2pair
* Holybro S500 V2 1045 self-locking propeller
* Holybro X500 V2 1045 self-locking propeller
* 1045 self-locking threaded-hub propeller
* T1045 self-locking propeller for 2216 920KV motors

## Required Propeller Size

* 1045
* 10x4.5
* CW and CCW included
* At least two pairs per full quad set

## Quantity To Order

Order at least 3 full quad sets

That means:

* 12 total propellers
* 6 CW propellers
* 6 CCW propellers

## Known Motor Directions

* Front right motor: CCW
* Back right motor: CW
* Back left motor: CCW
* Front left motor: CW

## Propeller Placement

* Front left: CW prop
* Back right: CW prop
* Front right: CCW prop
* Back left: CCW prop

## Unsafe Methods Rejected

Do not use:

* Nuts under the propeller
* Nuts forced into the propeller hub
* Glue
* Tape
* Drilled propeller hubs
* Random hardware-store nuts
* Props that wobble
* Props that cannot be clamped or threaded securely
* Props where the nut only catches one or two threads

## Resume After Replacement

After correct propellers are installed, resume with the read-only manual flight plan before attempting any autonomous movement commands
